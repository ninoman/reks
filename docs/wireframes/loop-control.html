Quick loop: simpler, rekordbox-style

Today's block has seven controls (IN, OUT, ½, size, 2×, LOOP, RELOOP). rekordbox gets by with two small panels: size with ‹ › (½ and 2×; tap the size to loop, it turns amber while looping) and IN · OUT over RELOOP, which reads EXIT while looping. A strip under the panel switches between them. Below: today, the literal rekordbox panel, and three ways to fit that model into our deck. Every button works: click to try it, or pick a state for all of them.

Shared by A–D: no LOOP button (the size is the loop button), RELOOP and EXIT are one button, amber means a loop is on. Keys don't change: loop, reloop, loopIn, loopOut, loopResize keep their actions.

Open questions

  1. Default size. rekordbox starts at 16 beats here; we start at the last size used (4 by default). Keep ours?
  2. B and A: which page after IN? Pressing the IN key while on BEAT could flip the deck to IN / OUT, so OUT is one click away. Or leave the page alone.
  3. IN / OUT while looping. Today they move the loop's start or end; rekordbox lights them amber for that. Keep, amber in every option.
  4. 4-deck. Rows are 20 / 26 / 26 there: B's tabs and D's IN · OUT get the 20 px row; check they read at that size.
