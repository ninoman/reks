Mix view: the 3-band mixer

Wireframe for issue #56. The 3-band EQ layout, Simple and Advanced, next to today's 4-band Full (Advanced) and Simple views, drawn to scale from the tokens in src/styles/tokens.css and the rows of MixerPanel. Low fidelity: greys only, no vendor names. Nothing here is built. Revision 2 (2026-10-23) applies the maintainer's answers, listed in §6.

new in the 3-band views lit: a switch that is on, or any channel knob off-centre (every view) later, pending research empty mixer space (4 decks)

1 · The mixers side by side, to scale 1:1

Tops aligned, so the heights compare directly. Channels 2 and 3 have tracks (lit badges), as in the 2-deck default. The same example positions are set in every view: channel 2's LOW is cut and channel 3's MID is dipped, so those knobs are lit. In the 3-band views channel 2's FILTER is turned towards low-pass and channel 3's towards high-pass; 1 and 4 sit at centre (off, unlit).

2 · Mix panel at 1440 × 900, 2 decks

The whole window, same scale for every state. Decks take the mixer's height (--deck-h: var(--mixer-h), #44), so a shorter mixer gives the space back to the library; the waveform takes what is left in the deck. Row counts are whole 26 px library rows.

3 · Mix panel at 1440 × 900, 4 decks

Two compact decks stack beside the mixer on each side (--deck-h-compact 216 px, 440 px a side), so the mixer stays 440 px in every view and the library keeps 8 rows (5 at 1280 × 800). A shorter view gives its faders and the master meter up to 60 px (--fader-bonus, #46); the rest stays empty at the bottom of the mixer (hatched). Accepted for the 3-band views, which leave the most.

4 · Knob states: channel FILTER, and every channel knob lit

One bipolar FILTER knob per channel in the 4th knob slot (under LOW). Left of centre is low-pass, right is high-pass, centre is off with a detent (drags within a few degrees snap to it, double-click or Delete returns there, like the tempo fader). Every bipolar channel knob, in every view, 4-band included, lights the same way once it leaves centre: LEVEL, the EQ knobs and FILTER. So a channel that sounds different always shows why. Drawn at 3× of --knob-md (28 px).

5 · Effect select, PARAMETER and settings

The legend cell of the FILTER row becomes the effect select, one for all four channels, and a global PARAMETER knob sits under it in the legend column, left of the CUE caption and the dB scale. Neither costs height or width. With FILTER selected, PARAMETER sets the resonance (a global setting is no longer needed). The select lists FILTER only; more effects are later · pending research. #59 names echo and reverb as candidates that need a D21 note first.

Heights for the 4-band views are the tokens (--mixer-h-advanced / -simple / -simple-bare). 3-band Simple's rows add up to exactly 4-band Simple without filters, so it can reuse the 292 size class (mixerSize → "simple-bare"). 3-band Advanced is measured from the drawing and needs its own token. Waveform = deck height − 214; rows = ⌊(window height − 230 − mixer height) / 26⌋, both from research §6.

7 · Answers to the open UI questions (maintainer, 2026-10-23)

  1. Label: "3-band". Settings → Mixer, EQ: 4-band · 3-band.
  2. Crossfader: a setting, as for 4-band. Mixer view Simple / Advanced applies to both EQ layouts; 3-band Advanced shows the X-F assigns, crossfader and curve (§1, §2).
  3. Shortcuts: as proposed. No filter keys. X C V work only in Advanced, as today. Knobs stay keyboard reachable (arrows, Page Up/Down, Delete back to centre).
  4. Row caption: "Filter".
  5. Lit: every channel knob, in every view. LEVEL, EQ and FILTER light when off-centre, on the 4-band mixer too (§4).
  6. MID: set by the research task. #59 proposes one real bell at 1 kHz (not HM + LM together), −26 / +6 dB, Q 0.7, UNVERIFIED.
  7. Resonance: a PARAMETER knob under the effect select. Global, in the legend column (§5). Note: #59 proposed no PARAMETER knob in v1 and a fixed Q 1.4; this answer adds it. Its centre or default is where that Q would sit.
  8. Positions across layouts: start flat. Each layout keeps its own positions; the first switch to 3-band starts with every knob at centre.
  9. 4 decks: shown. §3 draws the empty space each view leaves at 4 decks.
