Mix view: the 3-band mixer

Wireframe for issue #56. The 3-band EQ layout, Simple and Advanced, next to today's 4-band Full (Advanced) and Simple views, drawn to scale from the tokens in src/styles/tokens.css and the rows of MixerPanel. Low fidelity: greys only, no vendor names. Nothing here is built. Revision 4 (2026-10-23): the maintainer's answers (§7) and the FX row from the EQ and effects spec (#59).

new in the 3-band views lit: a switch that is on, or any channel knob off-centre (every view)

1 · The mixers side by side, to scale 1:1

Tops aligned, so the heights compare directly. Channels 2 and 3 have tracks (lit badges), as in the 2-deck default. The same example positions are set in every view: channel 2's LOW is cut and channel 3's MID is dipped, so those knobs are lit. In the 3-band views channel 2's FILTER is turned towards low-pass and channel 3's towards high-pass; 1 and 4 sit at centre (off, unlit).

2 · Mix panel at 1440 × 900, 2 decks

The whole window, same scale for every state. Decks take the mixer's height (--deck-h: var(--mixer-h), #44), so a shorter mixer gives the space back to the library; the waveform takes what is left in the deck. Row counts are whole 26 px library rows.

3 · Mix panel at 1440 × 900, 4 decks

Two compact decks stack beside the mixer on each side (--deck-h-compact 216 px, 440 px a side), so the mixer stays 440 px in every view and the library keeps 8 rows (5 at 1280 × 800). A shorter view gives its faders and the master meter up to 60 px (--fader-bonus, #46), only as much as fits: 3-band Advanced gets the 47 px it has spare. Whatever is still spare goes into the spacing between the rows (maintainer, 2026-10-23), so the mixer always fills the 440 px and nothing sits empty: the strip columns and the legend lay out with justify-content: space-between, which keeps their rows lined up because each column has the same rows. Thin rules under the rows show the wider spacing.

4 · Knob states: channel FX, and every channel knob lit

One bipolar FX knob per channel in the 4th knob slot (under LOW); its row caption names the selected effect. With FILTER, left of centre is low-pass and right is high-pass (ECHO and REVERB: left mids/lows, right highs, #59 §3). Centre is off, with a dead zone of 0.485–0.515 as a detent (a drag that crosses centre sticks there; double-click or Delete returns there). Every bipolar channel knob, in every view, 4-band included, lights the same way once it leaves centre: LEVEL, the EQ knobs and FX. So a channel that sounds different always shows why. Drawn at 3× of --knob-md (28 px).

5 · FX row and settings

One mixer-wide row in the shared section, under the strips (above the crossfader in Advanced): the FX select as three buttons, FILTER · ECHO · REVERB, exactly one always lit (FILTER at start, no off state: each channel's knob at centre is off), and one PARAMETER knob whose caption follows the effect: RESONANCE, FEEDBACK, DECAY. The FX row caption in the strips follows it too. NOISE, CRUSH and SWEEP are not planned. Spec: #59, djm-eq-fx.md §3.

Heights for the 4-band views are the tokens (--mixer-h-advanced / -simple / -simple-bare). The 3-band heights are measured from the drawing (the FX row adds to the 292 px of the five knob rows) and each needs its own token. Waveform = deck height − 214; rows = ⌊(window height − 230 − mixer height) / 26⌋, both from research §6.

7 · Answers to the open UI questions (maintainer, 2026-10-23)

  1. Label: "3-band". Settings → Mixer, EQ: 4-band · 3-band.
  2. Crossfader: a setting, as for 4-band. Mixer view Simple / Advanced applies to both EQ layouts; 3-band Advanced shows the X-F assigns, crossfader and curve (§1, §2).
  3. Shortcuts: as proposed. No filter keys. X C V work only in Advanced, as today. Knobs stay keyboard reachable (arrows, Page Up/Down, Delete back to centre).
  4. Row caption: "Filter". Now the selected effect's name: FILTER, ECHO or REVERB.
  5. Lit: every channel knob, in every view. LEVEL, EQ and FILTER light when off-centre, on the 4-band mixer too (§4).
  6. MID: set by the research task. #59 proposes one real bell at 1 kHz (not HM + LM together), −26 / +6 dB, Q 0.7, UNVERIFIED.
  7. Resonance: the PARAMETER knob. Global, in the FX row with the FX select FILTER · ECHO · REVERB (§5, spec #59). With FILTER it is the resonance; with ECHO feedback, with REVERB decay.
  8. Positions across layouts: start flat. Each layout keeps its own positions; the first switch to 3-band starts with every knob at centre.
  9. 4 decks: shown, no empty space. §3: after the 60 px fader bonus, the spare height is spread over the row spacing.
