Mix view: 3-band + filter mixer

Wireframe for issue #56. The proposed third mixer view next to today's Full (Advanced) and Simple views, drawn to scale from the tokens in src/styles/tokens.css and the rows of MixerPanel. Low fidelity: greys only, no vendor names. Nothing here is built.

new in the 3-band + filter view lit (switch on, filter knob off-centre) option, only if open question 2 says yes later, pending research

1 · The mixers side by side, to scale 1:1

Tops aligned, so the heights compare directly. Channel 2 and 3 have tracks (lit badges), as in the 2-deck default. In the new view channel 2's filter is turned towards low-pass and channel 3's towards high-pass; 1 and 4 sit at centre (off).

2 · Mix panel at 1440 × 900, 2 decks

The whole window, same scale for every state. Decks take the mixer's height (--deck-h: var(--mixer-h), #44), so a shorter mixer gives the space back to the library; the waveform takes what is left in the deck. Row counts are whole 26 px library rows. With 4 decks the mixer stays 440 px in every view and its faders take up to 60 px more (--fader-bonus), as in #46.

3 · Channel FILTER knob states

One bipolar knob per channel in the 4th knob slot (under LOW). Left of centre is low-pass, right is high-pass, centre is off with a detent (drags within a few degrees snap to it, double-click or Delete returns there, like the tempo fader). Off-centre it is lit: the arc from centre and the cap glow, so a channel that sounds filtered always shows why. Drawn at 3× of --knob-md (28 px).

4 · Later: an effect on the 4th knob later · pending research

If the 4th knob later carries more than a filter, the least obtrusive place for the choice is the legend cell of that row: the FILTER caption becomes a small selector, one for all four channels (as hardware has one selector for the whole mixer). It costs no height and no width, and the knobs keep their meaning: centre off, both sides the effect. Which effects, and whether they fit a single bipolar knob, is for the research session; nothing beyond the filter is drawn.

Heights for Full and the Simple views are the tokens (--mixer-h-advanced / -simple / -simple-bare); the new view's rows add up to exactly Simple-without-filters, so it can reuse the 292 size class (mixerSize → "simple-bare") rather than add a token. The option's height is measured from the drawing; it would need its own token. Waveform = deck height − 214; rows = ⌊(window height − 230 − mixer height) / 26⌋, both from research §6.

6 · Open UI questions

  1. Label wording. No vendor names in UI strings. Candidates: "3-band + filter" (drawn), "3-band", "Channel filter". Also the setting's shape: one 3-way Mixer view (A) or a separate EQ setting (B), see §4.
  2. Crossfader: yes or no? Drawn without, as Simple (292 px, 14 rows). With one, the X-F assigns must come back too, or the crossfader does nothing: about 355 px and 12 rows, nearly Simple's 356 (dashed in §1 and §2). B above would give this as "Advanced + 3-band".
  3. Shortcuts. Today there are no filter keys (mixKeys.ts), so the view changes nothing there unless keys are added. X C V do nothing, as in Simple (mixKeyAction(…, crossfader = false)), and the Shortcuts list leaves them out. Proposal: no new keys; each knob is keyboard reachable (arrows, Page Up/Down, Delete back to centre).
  4. Row caption. "Filter" (drawn) for the knob row. The legend already says "Filter" for the assign row in Full, so the word changes meaning between views. "LPF / HPF" ends under the knob only in the enlarged drawing; is a caption-free knob enough at 28 px?
  5. Lit look. Drawn: arc from centre plus a lit cap and glow (lit, shadow-lit). Should the EQ knobs light when off-centre too, or only FILTER, since it is the one that changes the sound most?
  6. MID: one knob, which band? A real single bell, or HM + LM driven together. Pending the research session; the caption stays MID either way.
  7. Resonance. Fixed, or a global setting in Settings → Mixer (one row, no mixer space).
  8. Positions across views. Per #56, each layout keeps its own positions; hidden controls send neutral values (effectiveMix). Does HI / LOW carry over between 4-band and 3-band, or start flat?
  9. 4 decks. The mixer stays 440 and the faders take 60 px; the remaining 88 px sit empty at the bottom, as in Simple today. Acceptable?
