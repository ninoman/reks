Quick-loop block

The same seven controls in every concept, always on the deck whatever the pad mode. A row where the deck is wide and short of height, a 3 × 2 block beside the pads where it is short.

LOOP is lit while any loop plays (beat, manual, memory or auto loop). The readout shows the active loop's length, or dimmed, the size LOOP will use next.

Simple and advanced mixer

One setting chooses how much of the mixer shows, in every concept. Simple hides the filter assign switches, the crossfader and its assign switches; it can also hide both filter units. Use Mixer view above to see each frame both ways.
